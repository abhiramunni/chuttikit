02 · THE BRIDGE FINDER

Long weekends, ranked by value

A "bridge" is simple: a holiday sits next to a weekend, and one or two leave days stretch it into a real break. These are computed from the gazetted list and your work week — best value first, so the top card is the smartest leave day you will spend all year.

How this is worked out: for each holiday we look at the nearest working days before and after it, and take at most 2 of them as leave. The window shown is the longest unbroken run of days off that results. Moon-sighted holidays (Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Muharram, Milad-un-Nabi) can shift by a day — confirm near the date.

Kerala · 11–15 September

Onam week needs zero leave days

First Onam (Sat 11 Sep) to Sree Narayana Guru Jayanthi (Wed 15 Sep) is five days off in a row for offices on the Kerala state list — Thiruvonam itself is Sunday 12 September. If your office follows the Kerala list, this is the best free break of 2027.

Year-end · 25 December 2026

Don't wait for 2027 to start

Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday — an instant 3-day weekend, 28 days from now. Take Thursday 24 December off and it becomes 4 days. New Year's Day 2027 is also a Friday, so the year opens with another 3-day weekend.
